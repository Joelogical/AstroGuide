# Alan Leo: Planetary Principles

These are source-specific principles from Leo's esoteric framework. They
should be used as functions in a system, not as isolated personality
keywords.

## Sun

Central organizing life principle, individuality, vitality,
consciousness, and a solar center around which other functions are
coordinated. In Leo's esoteric model the Sun has special importance as
the expression of a unifying life principle.

## Moon

Responsive and personal consciousness, receptivity, habit, sensation,
inherited or conditioned response, and the personal vehicle through
which influences are received.

## Mercury

Mind, reason, mediation, communication, adaptation, translation, and
connection between levels of experience. Mercury frequently functions as
a bridge.

## Venus

Attraction, affinity, harmony, cohesion, refinement, beauty,
relationship, and unification. Leo gives Venus a higher unifying
significance beyond ordinary affection.

## Mars

Desire, force, activity, assertion, passion, initiative, and the
mobilization of personal energy.

## Jupiter

Expansion, preservation, growth, synthesis, inclusion, and broader
integration.

## Saturn

Contraction, limitation, boundary, crystallization, structure,
concentration, responsibility, testing, separation, and definition. Leo
also treats Saturn as a threshold or bridge, so it should not be reduced
to hardship.

### Saturn interpretation rule

Ask what Saturn is: - defining; - concentrating; - delaying; -
stabilizing; - limiting; - separating; - formalizing.

## Uranus

Individualization, independence, originality, awakening, unconventional
perception, higher will, and movement beyond established Saturnian
boundaries.

## Neptune

Subtle, psychic, mystical, diffuse, or transpersonal perception in Leo's
esoteric framework. His treatment is historically layered and should not
be flattened into a modern keyword list.

## Functional hierarchy

Leo often organizes planetary functions into levels rather than treating
every planet as equivalent. Preserve the concept of hierarchical
organization while labeling his metaphysical hierarchy as
source-specific.
