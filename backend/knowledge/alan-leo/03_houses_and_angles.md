# Alan Leo: Houses and Angles

## Sign and house are different dimensions

Leo distinguishes zodiacal quality from mundane field.

### Rule

-   Sign: mode or quality through which a planetary principle operates.
-   House: sphere or field in which it becomes manifest.

Do not produce redundant sign/house interpretations.

## Quadruplicity of houses

-   Angular: 1, 4, 7, 10
-   Succedent: 2, 5, 8, 11
-   Cadent: 3, 6, 9, 12

## Elemental house triplicities

-   Fire: 1, 5, 9
-   Earth: 2, 6, 10
-   Air: 3, 7, 11
-   Water: 4, 8, 12

## Angles

The Ascendant, IC, Descendant, and MC form a major organizing cross.

### Rule

A closely angular planet should receive greater structural attention
than an otherwise similar non-angular placement.

## Hemisphere and orientation

Leo uses eastern/western and above/below-horizon divisions as structural
modifiers.

### Rule

Treat hemisphere patterns as secondary modifiers. Do not convert them
into deterministic personality labels.

## House patterning

If multiple important factors activate houses from the same structural
group, treat the repetition as a chart-level pattern rather than
interpreting each house independently.
