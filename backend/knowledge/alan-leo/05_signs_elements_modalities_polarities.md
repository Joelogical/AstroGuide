# Alan Leo: Zodiacal Structure

## Zodiac as relational system

The signs are not twelve independent personality categories. Leo
organizes them through polarity, triplicity, quadruplicity, rulership,
and subdivision.

## Elements

-   Fire: activity, vitality, will, individualizing force
-   Earth: concreteness, embodiment, materialization
-   Air: mind, relation, communication, abstraction
-   Water: feeling, responsiveness, receptivity, psychic function

## Modalities

-   Cardinal: initiation, movement, differentiation
-   Fixed: concentration, persistence, consolidation, individualization
-   Mutable: transition, adaptation, mediation, redistribution

## Polarity

Leo uses positive/negative and masculine/feminine terminology typical of
his period.

### Modern output rule

Default to neutral functional language such as: - expressive/receptive -
outward/inward - projective/absorptive

Retain Leo's historical terms only when discussing his system directly.

## Polarity synthesis

Leo's Part II develops Sun-Moon polarities and relates them to the
Ascendant and to the personal/individual structure of the horoscope.

### Rule

If AstroGuide implements this doctrine, compute the underlying sign
polarities deterministically and let the knowledge layer interpret their
relationship. Do not ask the LLM to infer sign polarity from memory.

## Repetition

Elemental, modal, and polarity concentrations are structural patterns.
They should modify synthesis rather than become simplistic labels such
as "you are very Fire."
