# Alan Leo: Personality, Individuality, and Star Maps

## Source status

This module represents a distinctive esoteric technique developed in
Part II. It is not a standard astronomical calculation and should be
explicitly identified as Alan Leo's esoteric method.

## Personality and individuality

Leo distinguishes the temporary/personal organization from a more
enduring individuality within his Theosophical model.

The system uses zodiacal relationships and geometrical diagrams to
represent these levels.

## Star of the Personality

The signs and their geometrical arrangement play a prominent role in
describing the personal star. Leo connects this with personal coloring
and the form through which consciousness is expressed.

## Star of the Individuality

Leo develops a second star intended to symbolize the more enduring
individuality. His method treats the personal star as something that can
be transcended in spiritual development.

## Application rule

Do not tell a user that a star map objectively measures the "age of the
soul." If implemented, phrase it as:

"Within Alan Leo's esoteric system, this configuration is interpreted
as..."

## Computational implementation

Before implementing star maps: 1. encode Leo's geometry explicitly; 2.
test against the book's worked examples; 3. reproduce at least one
published example exactly; 4. keep the feature behind an `esoteric_leo`
method flag; 5. never let the LLM invent the geometry.

## Mrs. Annie Besant example

Leo uses the horoscope of Annie Besant as a worked example in Part II.
Worked examples should be used as validation cases for any future
implementation, not as templates for generic personality readings.
