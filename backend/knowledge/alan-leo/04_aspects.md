# Alan Leo: Aspect Framework

## Core rule

An aspect is a relationship between planetary functions. Its meaning
depends on the planets, geometry, condition, and chart context.

## Interpretation order

1.  planets involved;
2.  exact aspect type;
3.  orb;
4.  applying/separating status if calculated;
5.  signs;
6.  houses occupied;
7.  houses ruled;
8.  dignity/condition;
9.  angularity;
10. participation in larger configurations.

## Conjunction

Unifies and intensifies. Its quality depends on the planets and their
condition.

Do not classify conjunction as automatically harmonious or difficult.

## Opposition

Polarity. Can operate through complementarity, projection, separation,
oscillation, or eventual integration.

Do not reduce opposition to "conflict."

## Square

Dynamic incompatibility, pressure, friction, or forced adjustment. It
can mobilize development and events.

Do not reduce square to "challenge."

## Trine

Low-friction flow, harmony, and ready coordination.

Do not assume ease equals importance, virtue, or conscious development.

## Sextile

Constructive relationship with more activation or opportunity than the
trine in Leo's treatment.

## Semi-sextile

Minor relationship. Use only if enabled in the application's aspect
doctrine.

## Semi-square and sesquiquadrate

Secondary hard relationships derived from the square family. Use only if
enabled.

## Quincunx

Distinct, awkward, difficult-to-integrate relationship in Leo's
framework. Keep source-specific qualifications if the app uses a
different modern doctrine.

## Parallel

Leo treats parallels as conjunction-like relationships, qualified by the
planets and surrounding chart.

## Threefold and fourfold geometry

Leo relates harmonious aspects to threefold divisions of the circle and
hard aspects to twofold/fourfold divisions. This is part of his
philosophical explanation of aspect quality.

### Implementation boundary

The geometry is deterministic. Leo's metaphysical explanation of why
that geometry is harmonious or difficult is interpretive doctrine.

## Language

Prefer: - reinforcement - integration - friction - inhibition -
activation - polarization - separation - facilitation

Avoid treating "benefic/malefic" or "good/bad" as sufficient
interpretation.
