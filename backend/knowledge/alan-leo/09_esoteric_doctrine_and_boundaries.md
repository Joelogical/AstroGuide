# Alan Leo: Esoteric Doctrine and Boundaries

## Central doctrine

Leo's system is explicitly Theosophical. It connects astrology with: -
karma; - reincarnation; - spiritual evolution; - levels of
consciousness; - subtle bodies; - planetary intelligences; - the aura; -
occult cosmology; - the relationship of microcosm and macrocosm.

These are interpretive/metaphysical doctrines, not astronomical
observations.

## Karma and reincarnation

Leo states that his natal astrology is deeply dependent on karma and
reincarnation.

### Attribution

Do not state karma or reincarnation as established fact. Use
attribution: "Within Leo's framework..." "Leo interprets this as..." "In
this esoteric tradition..."

## Aura and subtle bodies

Leo uses a layered model of human consciousness and vehicles.

### Rule

Keep this material out of default psychological or technical chart
analysis unless the user requests esoteric interpretation.

## Gestation and heredity

Leo discusses planetary influence during gestation and heredity.

### Safety/epistemic rule

Do not present these claims as biological or medical facts.

## Angelic host / Devas / planetary spirits

These belong to Leo's occult cosmology.

### Rule

Treat them as historical-esoteric doctrine. Never represent them as
empirically established entities.

## Caste and social distinctions

Leo uses historical language about caste, development, and human
hierarchy.

### Rule

Do not reproduce this as a ranking of contemporary people or groups. If
relevant to source study, describe it as part of Leo's historical
Theosophical framework.

## Gendered terminology

Translate masculine/feminine sign language into functional polarity
language in default output.

## "Soul age"

Do not infer or declare a user's spiritual maturity or age of soul as an
objective fact.
