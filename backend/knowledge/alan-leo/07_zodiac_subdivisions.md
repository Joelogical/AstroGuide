# Alan Leo: Zodiacal Subdivisions

Part III develops increasingly fine divisions of the zodiac.

## Structural principle

Leo relates zodiacal divisions to threefold and fourfold schemes and to
a hierarchy of expression.

## Relevant subdivisions

The source discusses: - triplicity; - quadruplicity; - polarity; -
decanates; - septanates; - additional sign subdivisions; - relationships
among subdivisions, planes, and planetary principles.

## Decanates

A 30-degree sign can be divided into three 10-degree decanates.

### Deterministic rule

If used, compute decanate from exact longitude. Do not let the LLM
estimate it from degree text.

## Further subdivisions

Leo connects finer subdivisions with an esoteric hierarchy. These should
be stored as source-specific lookup data rather than universal chart
facts.

## Retrieval rule

Do not introduce decanates, septanates, or other fine subdivisions into
ordinary readings merely because they are available. Use them when: -
the user asks for them; - an advanced/esoteric analysis explicitly
enables them; - they materially clarify a configuration.

## Precision rule

Finer subdivisions require accurate birth data. The smaller the
subdivision, the more important input precision becomes.
