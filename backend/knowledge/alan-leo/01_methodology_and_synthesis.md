# Alan Leo: Methodology and Synthesis

## Horoscope as an integrated system

Leo's method is synthetic. A horoscope is not adequately interpreted by
adding independent placement meanings. Each factor is conditioned by the
whole figure.

### How Leo applies a factor

For any important factor, evaluate: 1. the planet or point; 2. sign
condition; 3. house field; 4. aspects; 5. angularity or structural
prominence; 6. rulership and dignity where the application supports
them; 7. repeated elemental, modal, polarity, or house patterns; 8. its
relationship to the dominant configuration.

## Hierarchy

Do not assign equal weight to all placements. Establish which factors
organize the chart and which merely modify it.

Prioritize, when available: - luminaries and Ascendant; - chart ruler; -
angular planets; - very close aspects; - repeated planetary
connections; - concentrated sign/house patterns; - planets acting as
links between several configurations; - strong dignity/debility within
the chosen doctrine.

## Aspects modify, rather than replace, planetary principles

Leo treats aspects as relationships through which planetary functions
combine, support, inhibit, separate, or condition each other.

### Rule

Interpret the planets first, then the relationship. Do not reduce an
aspect to a canned phrase.

## Contradiction is legitimate chart structure

A chart may contain mutually competing tendencies. Do not force them
into a single theme.

When factors conflict: 1. name the conflict; 2. compare their structural
weight; 3. explain the conditions under which each may become more
prominent; 4. identify any third factor that mediates the conflict.

## Character and environment

Leo distinguishes symbolism describing the native from symbolism
expressed through circumstances, associates, or environment.

### Rule

Do not psychologize every house or aspect. Preserve the possibility of
environmental manifestation when the chart context supports it.

## Developmental range

Leo frequently interprets configurations as capable of lower, ordinary,
and more developed expressions.

### Modern implementation

Treat this as a range of expression, not as a ranking of human worth.
Avoid declaring a user's spiritual level, caste, moral worth, or "age of
soul" as fact.

## Whole-chart conclusion

A final synthesis should state: - the dominant organizing factors; -
secondary supporting structures; - major contradictions; - unusually
concentrated or weakly represented functions; - the strongest links
among houses/planets; - the limits of the interpretation.

Do not end with a generic personality summary if the user requested
chart analysis.
